Problem

  • Visa eligibility is hiddenKorean job posts rarely say which visas can apply.
  • Language barriersPosts, employer messages and contracts are in Korean only.
  • Hour limits are easy to breakVisa part-time hour caps are easy to exceed without noticing.

Features

  • Daily job picksMatched by visa and city.
  • Visa-fit labelsEach job is labelled for how well it fits your visa.
  • Part-time hour guardWarns before visa hour limits are exceeded.
  • Halal & mosque finderNearby halal food and mosques.
  • AI Korean message draftsPolite Korean messages to employers in one tap.
  • Application trackerEvery application and its status in one place.
  • Instant alertsNew matching jobs as soon as they appear.
  • Twice-weekly tailored newsletterPersonalised jobs and news every Tuesday and Friday.
  • Hamroh invite gameInvite friends and grow the community together.

Stack

Telegram bot and mini app on Cloudflare Workers, D1 and Workers AI.

Built with a team of AI agents that I direct, covering research, engineering, design and outreach.
