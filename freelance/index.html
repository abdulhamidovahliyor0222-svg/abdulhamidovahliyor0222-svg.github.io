Case study: Mehnatkash Korea

Founder & Builder · September 2026 – present

I designed and built Mehnatkash Korea, a free Telegram job bot and Mini App that helps foreign workers and international students in Korea find jobs in Uzbek, Russian, English and Korean. It pulls listings from official public job sources (Work24 / 고용24 Open API on data.go.kr, ALIO) and other job boards, then presents them in simple button menus. For student-visa (D-2/D-4) users, keyword rules hide construction, factory and other heavy-labour roles. It runs on Cloudflare Workers and D1 on the free plan. I built it end to end: the data pipeline, bot logic, multilingual UX, filtering rules, tests and deployment.

The problem

Job listings in Korea are spread across Korean-only sites, and newcomers often can’t tell whether a job fits their visa.

The solution

  • Visa-aware job matching by city, visa and language
  • Daily personal job alerts with a one-tap “turn off” button
  • Tue/Fri tailored newsletter, sent in rate-safe batches
  • Telegram Mini App: job list, filters, city picker, student hub
  • Visa notes always say “not legal advice: confirm with 1345 / HiKorea”

Architecture

  • Cloudflare Workers: bot webhook, Mini App, cron jobs for collectors, alerts and newsletter
  • Cloudflare D1: users, preferences, listings, send progress
  • Workers AI: extraction and translation. Rules make the visa decisions, and the LLM only extracts.
  • Feature flags: admin-only rollout first, then publish to everyone
